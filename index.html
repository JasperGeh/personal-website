I am a Senior Research Scientist in SecureBio's AI team, where my research focuses on AI evaluations for biosecurity. Previously, I was working on far-UVC air disinfection as a Research Fellow at Convergent Research, and completed my virology PhD at the Hannover Medical School.

Outside of work, you can find me reading, running TTRPGs, hiking, or playing the drums.
